# Effects Presets

## Principle

Every effect needs a job. Do not add motion just to make the timeline look busy.

## Standard Toolkit

- **Punch-in**: important claim, emotional beat, CTA, strong insight. Hold at least 3 seconds unless intentionally used as a very quick hit.
- **Smooth zoom**: long explanation sections where the footage would feel static.
- **Hard snap emphasis**: strong words such as "ทำได้แล้ว", "ไม่ต้องติดคนตัดต่อ", "ไม่ถึง 10 นาที".
- **B-roll insert**: product, screen, before/after, chart, example, proof.
- **Split-screen/PiP**: speaker plus product/demo/chart at the same time.
- **Keyword pop**: one large phrase that anchors the idea.
- **SFX**: pop, whoosh, click, impact. Use sparingly.
- **Color grade**: subtle cinematic polish, skin-safe contrast, light vignette/grain.

## Timing Density

- Aim for a meaningful visual change every 5-8 seconds.
- Use B-roll when the speaker references an object, screen, result, number, or example.
- Use keyword emphasis for a few important phrases per minute, not every sentence.

## Avoid By Default

- Heavy shake, spin, glitch, and fast strobe.
- Zooms that crop the speaker's mouth/chin unless asked.
- Repeating the same animation pattern so often that it becomes noise.
